# Credits

Threads of History's own data (events, links, actors, paths, scenarios and the rest of `data/`) is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (see `LICENSE`); the code is under the MIT licence (`LICENSE-CODE`). Two kinds of material are not ours to dedicate, and keep their own terms:

- **Quotes.** The `quote` fields in `sources` are short excerpts from the cited works, included to show what each source says. They remain the property of their authors and publishers.
- **Imported tables** in `data/tables/` and the series that describe them come from the datasets below, under the licences shown. Credit them as given here when you use their tables. In particular, tables from V-Dem (`vdem-*`) are under CC BY-SA 4.0: anything shared that is built from them must use the same licence. Tables kept outside git for licence reasons (`.cache/private-tables/`) are not distributed.

This file is generated from `data/datasets/` by `python tools/credits.py`.
